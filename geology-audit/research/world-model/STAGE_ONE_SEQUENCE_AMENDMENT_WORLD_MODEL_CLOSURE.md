# Stage 1 Sequence Amendment — Research First, Causal Redesign After

## Decision

Stage 1 geological research will continue to completion before WorldWright begins generator redesign or implementation of the Minimum Causal Core.

The World Model Completeness Audit remains valid and important, but it does **not** replace or pause the remaining geological research domains.

The correct order is:

```text
finish the complete geological research corpus
→ finalize generator obligations from that research
→ revisit and harden the Minimum Causal Core
→ produce the implementation roadmap
→ implement the causal world model in bounded PRs
→ validate the causal generator
→ begin Stage 2 reference-image generation only after explicit approval
```

This document supersedes the earlier instruction that volcanism research was paused after the collision/orogeny tranche.

## Why research must finish first

The architecture audit established that the live generator lacks enough causal state and interaction to produce many realistic outcomes. That diagnosis is correct.

However, the exact replacement architecture cannot be finalized responsibly until Stage 1 has researched the remaining systems, including:

- volcanism and magmatic construction;
- ocean margins, shelves, slopes, and basin architecture;
- rivers, erosion, sediment transport, and deposition;
- glacial, aeolian, coastal, and karst processes;
- impacts and resurfacing;
- climate–geology interaction;
- temporal evolution and multiscale coherence;
- procedural failures and valid exceptions.

Those domains may reveal additional required state, relationships, conserved quantities, and process fields. Implementing the causal core before finishing them could create another simplified architecture that later proves incomplete.

## Role of the World Model Completeness Audit during research

The audit now serves as a cross-cutting research lens.

It requires every remaining geological domain to answer both:

1. what the real geological system does and how it appears; and
2. what WorldWright must know, preserve, route, and transmit to produce it.

The audit documents already committed remain first drafts and working hypotheses. They must be revised after the complete research corpus exposes the full set of generator obligations.

## Required output for every remaining research domain

Every domain must produce two linked specifications.

### A. Geological and visual specification

- physical causes;
- system components and relationships;
- expected morphology;
- threshold behavior;
- parameter interactions;
- planetary, continental, regional, and local expression;
- valid morphological diversity;
- exceptions and ambiguous analogues;
- temporal evolution and overprinting;
- procedural failure signatures;
- future Stage 2 visual-reference obligations.

### B. Generator obligation specification

- required canonical state;
- required upstream dependencies;
- required geological-history or event records;
- required graphs, process fields, and material layers;
- required downstream consequences;
- quantities that must be conserved, routed, or reconciled;
- acceptable bounded approximations;
- invalid shortcuts and forbidden reverse authority;
- provenance and diagnostic requirements;
- future Stage 2 causal-reference and test obligations.

A domain is not `SPEC COMPLETE` if it documents only what the final feature should look like.

## What is allowed before research completion

Allowed:

- reading and auditing the live generator;
- documenting architectural gaps;
- refining research schemas and terminology;
- identifying provisional canonical records;
- adding research-only generator obligations;
- preserving diagnostic and provenance requirements.

Not allowed:

- implementing the causal generator redesign;
- deleting or replacing the current generator path;
- tuning new visual terrain patches as substitutes for missing systems;
- creating reference images;
- converting provisional research thresholds into numeric CI gates;
- treating the first-draft Minimum Causal Core as final.

## Stage 2 remains blocked

No reference-image generation may begin until:

- every required Stage 1 domain is `SPEC COMPLETE` or intentionally excluded with justification;
- both visual and generator obligations are complete;
- the full Stage 2 coverage matrix contains no unexplained gaps;
- source and asset licensing requirements are resolved;
- the post-research causal architecture and implementation sequence are approved;
- the user explicitly approves moving to Stage 2.
