## Main scientific conclusions

### 1. Volcanism is a history, not an activity scalar

The minimum causal chain is:

```text
heat and mantle/crust state
→ melt generation
→ transport and storage
→ intrusive/extrusive partition
→ vent and fissure selection
→ eruption and deposits
→ construction, loading, collapse, erosion, burial, and resurfacing
→ visible volcanic terrain
```

### 2. Magma supply and erupted volume are different

A volcanic system may store or crystallize much of its magma below the surface. Intrusion can change crustal thickness, density, rheology, heat, metamorphism, and later uplift or subsidence even when little lava is erupted.

### 3. Edifice families are outcomes rather than generator presets

Shield, composite, cone, dome, caldera, volcanic field, lava plain, and plateau morphology depend on supply history, rheology, vent geometry, environment, substrate, gravity, erosion, and collapse.

### 4. Calderas require collapse history

A caldera is not a large crater stamp. It requires a relation to magma storage/withdrawal, structural failure, collapse style, later infill, resurgence, water/sediment state, and nested activity.

### 5. Collapse must preserve mass consequences

Sector collapse and caldera failure cannot delete terrain without a destination. Removed mass must appear as debris-avalanche, pyroclastic, lahar, submarine, sedimentary, or explicitly exported material.

### 6. Hotspot tracks record relative motion

Age-progressive chains reflect motion between the source and the plate. Sources are not universally fixed. Broad swells, volcanic construction, underplating, cooling/subsidence, and flexure are separate contributions.

### 7. Large igneous provinces are distributed systems

LIPs contain fissures, stacked flows, dikes, sills, intrusions, underplating, pulses, host-rock interaction, and later erosion/fragmentation. They must not become one giant volcano or uniform circular lava disk.

### 8. Planetary volcanism cannot be gravity-rescaled Earth

Gravity, atmosphere, water/ice, lithosphere, tectonic regime, plate motion, burial, erosion, and impact history interact. Mars, Venus, Io, the Moon, Mercury, and Earth require labeled regime branches.

### 9. Radial volcanic forms are valid exceptions

Valid cause-backed radiality may include:

- shield edifices;
- summit calderas and ring faults;
- radial dike/rift systems;
- volcanic swells;
- circumferential faults;
- local radial drainage;
- flexural moats;
- corona- or patera-like systems.

These forms remain valid only when scale, source, component-specific geometry, history, material budget, asymmetry, and downstream consequences are explicit.

## Generator obligations discovered

Future WorldWright architecture must be able to carry at least:

- volcanic source/province identity;
- source setting and confidence/model branch;
- melt-generation and magma-supply history;
- intrusive/extrusive partition;
- vent/fissure/plumbing topology;
- construction and deposit layers;
- caldera and sector-collapse events;
- load and flexural response;
- surface-age reset and burial;
- erosion and sediment handoff;
- ocean/ice/atmosphere context;
- active, waning, fossil, buried, eroded, and rejuvenated states;
- normalized magma and preserved-material budgets.

These are research findings, not implementation authorization.
