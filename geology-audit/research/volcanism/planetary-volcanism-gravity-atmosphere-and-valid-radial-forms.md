# Planetary volcanism, gravity, atmosphere, and valid radial forms

## Research status

- **Domain IDs:** M02–M05, F01, F03, F07–F08, G02–G05, X04
- **Status:** IN RESEARCH
- **Stage 2 generation:** BLOCKED
- **Generator implementation:** DEFERRED UNTIL STAGE 1 RESEARCH COMPLETION
- **Purpose:** establish which volcanic relationships transfer across rocky worlds, which are Earth-specific, and which radial forms are legitimate geological outcomes rather than procedural artifacts.

## 1. Central rule

Planetary volcanism is controlled by more than magma composition.

Important controls include:

- gravity;
- atmospheric pressure and density;
- surface temperature;
- water or ice presence;
- lithosphere thickness and strength;
- crustal composition and density;
- internal and tidal heat;
- tectonic regime;
- eruption rate and duration;
- volatile inventory;
- erosion and weathering;
- plate motion or lack of plate motion;
- preservation time.

Earth volcano families cannot be copied directly to Venus, Mars, Io, the Moon, Mercury, or arbitrary rocky worlds by resizing them.

## 2. Gravity

Gravity influences:

- buoyancy and magma ascent pressure gradients;
- edifice load and lithospheric stress;
- maximum sustainable relief;
- ballistic tephra trajectories;
- plume rise and collapse;
- lava-flow thickness and runout;
- landslide behavior;
- flexural response;
- erosion and sediment transport.

### Lower-gravity tendency

Potential outcomes include:

- taller and broader edifices where supply and lithosphere permit;
- longer ballistic trajectories;
- different collapse and slope-stability thresholds;
- larger-scale relief preservation where erosion is weak.

### Higher-gravity tendency

Potential outcomes include:

- stronger load stress for a given edifice mass;
- potentially lower sustainable relief;
- shorter ballistic transport;
- stronger gravitational spreading/collapse tendency;
- different flow and flexure scaling.

No direct inverse-gravity height formula is approved. Lithosphere strength, eruption rate, plate motion, material rheology, and erosion can dominate.

## 3. Atmospheric pressure and density

Atmosphere affects:

- gas expansion and fragmentation;
- eruption columns;
- ash transport;
- pyroclastic density currents;
- lava cooling;
- volatile retention;
- wind redistribution;
- surface oxidation and weathering.

### Dense-atmosphere branch

A dense atmosphere may suppress or reshape explosive expansion, enhance aerodynamic transport of fine particles, and alter cooling and plume behavior. Venusian volcanic morphology cannot be treated as an airless-body or Earth clone.

### Thin-atmosphere branch

A thin atmosphere changes plume dynamics and ash transport and may permit different ballistic dominance. Mars requires a distinct eruption/deposit branch.

### Airless branch

On the Moon or Mercury:

- no ordinary atmospheric eruption column exists;
- ballistic emplacement and vacuum degassing dominate;
- there is little weathering or fluvial erosion;
- volcanic forms may persist for long periods unless buried, impacted, or tectonically modified.

## 4. Surface temperature and lava rheology

Cooling rate depends on:

- atmospheric conditions;
- substrate temperature;
- flow thickness;
- eruption rate;
- composition and crystallization;
- water/ice interaction;
- radiative and convective heat loss.

Extremely hot surfaces may change cooling and preservation, but they do not remove the need for supply, vent, and substrate controls.

## 5. Water and ice interaction

Water/ice may produce:

- phreatomagmatic explosions;
- tuff rings and maars;
- hyaloclastite;
- pillow lavas;
- subglacial tuyas;
- jökulhlaups and meltwater erosion;
- hydrothermal alteration;
- rapid fragmentation and sediment production;
- submarine pressure effects.

The same magma can create very different forms under deep water, shallow water, ice, or dry land.

## 6. Tectonic-regime controls

### Mobile-lid worlds

Volcanism may be organized into:

- ridges;
- arcs;
- rifts;
- backarcs;
- hotspots and intraplate fields;
- collision-related and post-collisional provinces.

### Stagnant-lid worlds

Potential behavior:

- long-lived plume or mantle-upwelling volcanism;
- broad volcanic rises;
- lithosphere-thickness filtering;
- localized or distributed resurfacing;
- intrusive accumulation;
- contractional or extensional overprinting without Earth-style plate networks.

### Episodic/deformable-lid worlds

Potential behavior:

- pulses of distributed magmatism and deformation;
- widespread plains resurfacing;
- coronae or volcanic-tectonic structures;
- regional rises and rifts;
- changing preservation ages.

### Heat-pipe/tidally heated worlds

Potential behavior:

- very high volcanic burial rates;
- widespread active centers;
- foundering or subsidence under accumulating deposits;
- weak preservation of old surfaces;
- global or hemispheric heat patterns.

## 7. Mars

Mars demonstrates that very large shield volcanoes can arise where:

- gravity is lower than Earth;
- lithosphere may be thick and strong;
- plate motion is absent or limited;
- magma repeatedly reaches a similar region;
- erosion and resurfacing differ from Earth.

Important caution:

Olympus Mons is not explained by low gravity alone. Long-lived focused supply, lithosphere, lack of plate translation, flank processes, and preservation all matter.

Potential Martian volcanic families:

- giant shields;
- broad volcanic rises;
- paterae and calderas;
- fissure systems;
- lava plains;
- rootless cones or water/ice-interaction forms;
- highly eroded or buried ancient constructs.

## 8. Venus

Venus contains widespread volcanic plains, shields, rises, coronae, rifts, and large edifices under a dense, hot atmosphere and a non-Earth tectonic regime.

Magellan radar comparisons have provided evidence interpreted as surface changes and recent/ongoing lava emplacement at several sites. These observations support active or geologically recent volcanism but do not make every radar-bright flow young.

Potential Venusian branches:

- broad shield and lava-flow systems;
- clustered small shields;
- volcanic plains;
- corona-associated volcanism and deformation;
- rift–rise systems;
- large edifices with summit calderas;
- intrusive/deformable-lid provinces;
- resurfacing mosaics rather than one global reset.

Generator obligation:

- Venus-style volcanism must interact with crustal rheology and distributed deformation rather than be painted onto an Earth plate map.

## 9. Io

Io is a tidal-heating end member with extensive active volcanism and very high resurfacing.

Potential expressions:

- widespread volcanic centers;
- large lava fields;
- paterae;
- plume deposits;
- rapid burial and color/material changes;
- mountains not necessarily built as volcanic cones;
- heat-flow distribution linked to tidal dissipation and interior transport.

Long-lived activity and volatile loss imply that surface age, burial, and atmospheric escape must be considered together.

WorldWright consequence:

- a high-volcanism world may have fewer preserved classic edifices because rapid resurfacing buries them.

## 10. Moon and Mercury

Volcanism on airless bodies may produce:

- basaltic plains filling impact basins;
- sinuous rilles;
- volcanic domes;
- pyroclastic deposits;
- vents and pits;
- intrusive uplift or floor-fractured structures;
- ancient, highly preserved flow fields modified by impacts.

Impact-basin topography strongly controls lava emplacement. Volcanic plains cannot be studied independently from impact history.

## 11. Valid radial and circular volcanic forms

Volcanism can legitimately create radial or circular patterns.

### Valid examples

- shield edifice;
- broad volcanic swell;
- summit caldera;
- ring-fault system;
- radial dike swarm;
- circumferential faults;
- corona or annular volcanic-tectonic system;
- radial drainage on an isolated edifice;
- flexural moat around a large load;
- patera or collapse depression;
- lava flows diverging from a central vent.

### Required distinction from procedural blobs

A radial form is valid only when:

- scale is explicit;
- source and structure are explicit;
- component geometries differ appropriately;
- asymmetry is allowed;
- construction/collapse history exists;
- material and volume are accounted for;
- downstream flexure, drainage, erosion, and burial are compatible;
- the radiality does not leak into unrelated continent, ocean, or province fields.

## 12. Non-radial volcanic forms

Volcanism also commonly produces:

- linear fissure systems;
- arcuate volcanic arcs;
- en echelon vents;
- distributed fields;
- plateau-like sheets;
- long age-progressive chains;
- fault-controlled clusters;
- rift-parallel ridges;
- irregular caldera complexes.

A generator that assumes volcanism is radial by default will be wrong across many major settings.

## 13. Preservation and apparent volcanic density

The number of visible volcanoes depends on:

- eruption rate;
- burial rate;
- erosion;
- sedimentation;
- tectonic recycling;
- impact gardening;
- glaciation;
- sea-level exposure;
- mapping resolution.

High volcanic activity does not always produce a densely cratered surface. It may produce smooth young plains through burial.

## 14. Multiscale visual obligations

### Planetary scale

- volcanic distribution matches heat and tectonic regime;
- resurfacing age patterns are plausible;
- gravity/atmosphere/water branches affect broad morphology and preservation;
- giant forms remain proportionate to planet and lithosphere context.

### Continental/basin scale

- rises, plains, plateaus, chains, arcs, fissure provinces, and impact-controlled fills;
- deformation and support appropriate to the world regime.

### Regional scale

- edifices, calderas, coronae, paterae, flow fields, fissures, vents, and collapse systems.

### Local scale

- lava textures, pits, cones, domes, rilles, ash/plume deposits, erosional or impact overprints.

## 15. Threshold and interaction axes

Future Stage 2 cases must cross:

- gravity;
- atmospheric pressure/density;
- surface temperature;
- volatile inventory;
- water/ice context;
- tectonic regime;
- lithosphere thickness/strength;
- internal versus tidal heating;
- plate motion;
- erosion/burial/impact rate;
- active versus fossil state.

No cross-planet universal scaling law is approved yet.

## 16. Generator obligation specification

### Canonical state required

- gravity and atmosphere parameters;
- water/ice/environmental context;
- tectonic/lid regime;
- lithosphere state;
- volcanic heat/source distribution;
- source and vent histories;
- construction/deposit state;
- resurfacing/burial rate;
- erosion/impact modification;
- confidence and analogue limitations.

### Required process fields

- melt generation;
- transport/vent localization;
- construction/deposition;
- explosive/ballistic/plume behavior class;
- load/support response;
- cooling/preservation;
- resurfacing and burial;
- interaction with water, ice, atmosphere, and impacts.

### Downstream consequences

- surface age;
- crust thickness/material;
- topography and bathymetry;
- atmospheric/ocean forcing;
- drainage or lack thereof;
- erosion/impact overprint;
- final material/color only after physical state exists.

### Approximation limits

- Earth morphology may seed a relationship family, not a dimension template;
- gravity scaling must be crossed with lithosphere, supply, and erosion;
- atmospheric effects may use ordinal regimes before full fluid modeling;
- airless, dense-atmosphere, thin-atmosphere, and Earthlike branches must remain distinct.

## 17. Procedural failure signatures

### 17.1 Earth volcano rescale
An Earth edifice is enlarged or shrunk only by gravity.

### 17.2 Low gravity equals giant shield
No supply, plate-motion, lithosphere, or preservation explanation.

### 17.3 All volcanism radial
Arcs, fissures, fields, and plateaus become circular bumps.

### 17.4 Dense atmosphere ignored
Venus-like worlds use Earth ash and cooling behavior unchanged.

### 17.5 Airless weathering
Lunar/Mercurian volcanic surfaces receive fluvial or atmospheric erosion without water/atmosphere authority.

### 17.6 Activity equals visible cone density
Rapidly resurfaced worlds accumulate unlimited preserved edifices.

### 17.7 Planetary analogues mixed
Mars, Venus, Io, and Earth features are blended without regime labels.

### 17.8 Valid radiality rejected
Any circular volcanic structure is flagged as a procedural blob despite explicit cause and scale.

### 17.9 Radiality leakage
A valid local shield or corona causes continent-scale circular elevation or ocean ghosts.

## 18. Stage 2 coverage obligations

Future references must eventually include:

- Earthlike mobile-lid volcanism;
- Mars-like giant shield and volcanic-rise branches;
- Venus-like plains, rises, shields, corona-associated and deformable-lid volcanism;
- Io-like high-resurfacing tidal volcanism;
- lunar/Mercurian basin-fill and airless-flow branches;
- dense-, thin-, Earthlike-, and airless-atmosphere comparisons;
- low-, moderate-, and high-gravity comparisons with constant and varying lithosphere/supply;
- valid radial and non-radial volcanic families;
- active, buried, eroded, impact-modified, and fossil states.

### Negative controls

- gravity-only scaling;
- all-radial volcanism;
- Earthlike atmosphere everywhere;
- preserved cone overload on resurfacing worlds;
- mixed analogue labels;
- false rejection of cause-backed radial forms;
- local radiality leaking into global masks.

## 20. Conclusions safe enough to carry forward

### High confidence

- volcanic morphology and preservation depend on gravity, atmosphere, water/ice, lithosphere, heat regime, plate motion, and time;
- lower gravity alone does not explain giant shields;
- stagnant-, episodic-, mobile-, and heat-pipe-like regimes produce different volcanic organization;
- rapid resurfacing can reduce preservation of individual edifices;
- radial volcanic forms are valid when source, scale, history, and consequences are explicit;
- planetary analogues must remain labeled rather than blended.

### Model-dependent or incomplete

- quantitative cross-planet scaling;
- Venusian tectonic/volcanic interpretations;
- Io heat-transport geometry;
- plume/source depth;
- atmosphere-dependent eruption thresholds.

### Not approved

- gravity-only rescaling;
- generic planetary volcano presets;
- treating all radial forms as errors;
- generator implementation;
- image generation.
