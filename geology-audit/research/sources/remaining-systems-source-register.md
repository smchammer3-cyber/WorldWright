# Remaining Stage 1 systems source register

## Scope

This register supports the coordinated deep pass for:

- glaciers, ice sheets, permafrost, meltwater, and glacioisostasy;
- aeolian transport, dunes, dust, deflation, and yardangs;
- coastal waves, tides, sediment cells, barriers, cliffs, reefs, and relative sea level;
- karst, groundwater, caves, dissolution, springs, and collapse;
- impact cratering, ejecta, shock, basins, crustal modification, and chronology;
- climate–orography, weathering, erosion, volcanic/impact forcing, and feedbacks;
- burial, exhumation, reactivation, event ordering, and landscape memory;
- multiscale coherence, numerical/procedural failure, provenance, and diagnostics.

**All entries remain citation-only during Stage 1. No external figure, photograph, map, DEM, simulation frame, mission product, or dataset is approved for Stage 2 reuse without asset-specific licensing review.**

## Source-use rules established by the coordinated deep pass

1. **Ice, wind, waves, groundwater, and impacts are material/energy systems, not textures.**
2. **Current appearance cannot uniquely determine formation process or age.**
3. **Atmosphere, gravity, target material, water/ice, and history must modify every planetary process branch.**
4. **Erosion, dissolution, excavation, collapse, and retreat require material destinations.**
5. **Glacial and coastal relative sea level require both water/ice transfer and vertical land response.**
6. **Karst requires coupled surface/subsurface topology and chemistry.**
7. **Impact radiality is valid only with event, target, ejecta, crustal, and degradation provenance.**
8. **Climate is a forcing and feedback system, not a direct terrain painter.**
9. **Material, structure, exposure, and activity ages are distinct.**
10. **Scale ownership, topology, conservation, provenance, and staged diagnostics are mandatory.**
11. **All external sources and assets remain citation-only pending exact rights review.**
