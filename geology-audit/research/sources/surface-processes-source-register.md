# Surface-processes source register

## Scope

This register supports Stage 1 research for:

- drainage-network initiation and integration;
- bedrock incision and transient river profiles;
- drainage-divide migration and capture;
- weathering, regolith, hillslope transport, and mass wasting;
- sediment entrainment, transport, sorting, storage, and source-to-sink routing;
- depositional basins and compaction;
- floodplains, avulsion, alluvial fans, deltas, and alluvial plains;
- Earth and non-Earth surface-process branches;
- generator obligations for water, sediment, material, event, and provenance state.

The evidence-state labels follow `source-register.md`.

**Every source remains citation-only during Stage 1. No figure, photograph, DEM, satellite image, map, experiment frame, or dataset is approved for Stage 2 reuse without asset-specific licensing review.**

## Source-use rules established by this tranche

1. **Rivers are active terrain-forming systems.** They cannot be draped over completed terrain.
2. **Drainage basins are mutable.** Divides migrate, channels capture, lakes spill, and outlets reorganize.
3. **Stream power is a bounded approximation.** Thresholds, lithology, width, flood variability, and sediment tools/cover must be preserved.
4. **Weathering precedes much sediment transport.** Regolith thickness, material, and strength must be explicit.
5. **Erosion requires a material destination.** Removed solid mass must enter sediment, dissolved load, storage, deposition, or explicit export.
6. **Sediment supply and transport capacity are separate.** Their balance determines incision, cover, aggradation, and bypass.
7. **Storage transforms signals.** Floodplains, fans, terraces, lakes, and basins delay and filter source changes.
8. **Accommodation matters.** Sediment supply alone does not determine basin fill.
9. **Avulsion is essential.** Floodplains, fans, and deltas require active/abandoned pathways and lobe switching.
10. **Radial fans are valid only with an apex, slope change, supply, and lobe history.**
11. **Planetary process rules are medium- and regime-specific.** Earth rivers, Titan channels, Martian paleorivers, and airless regolith cannot share one template.
12. **Every source remains citation-only until asset review.**
