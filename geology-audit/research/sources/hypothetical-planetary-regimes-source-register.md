# Hypothetical planetary regimes source register

## Scope

This register supports Stage 1 research for:

- mobile, stagnant, episodic, deformable/squishy, heat-pipe, and contraction-dominated regimes;
- regime transitions, hysteresis, and path dependence;
- waterworlds, drowned continents, freeboard, and seafloor pressure;
- dense, thin, and absent atmospheres;
- tidal heating, tidal locking, and hemispheric forcing;
- ice-shell ocean worlds and cryovolcanism;
- high- and low-gravity rocky worlds;
- impact chronology and resurfacing age;
- confidence labels and physically constrained hypothetical combinations;
- generator state, history, provenance, and contradiction rules.

**All entries remain citation-only during Stage 1. No figure, image, map, radar product, simulation frame, or dataset is approved for Stage 2 reuse without asset-specific licensing review.**

## Source-use rules established by this tranche

1. **Base regimes and overlays are separate.** Waterworld, high gravity, tidal heating, and atmosphere are not substitutes for tectonic regime.
2. **History is state.** Present inputs cannot uniquely define a planet without regime, resurfacing, impact, burial, and volatile history.
3. **Confidence must be encoded.** Observed, inferred, model-supported, extrapolated, speculative, and forbidden branches cannot be flattened.
4. **Mass alone does not determine tectonic regime.** Competing super-Earth models require probabilistic, history-aware logic.
5. **Freeboard must be solved.** Water-rich planets cannot be generated by simply flooding a finished Earthlike map.
6. **Tidal heating must be spatial and historical.** It cannot be a uniform volcano-density multiplier.
7. **Heat-pipe worlds require burial and vertical recycling.** Volcano count alone is insufficient.
8. **Ice-shell worlds need ice mechanics.** Rocky tectonic and erosion rules do not transfer directly.
9. **Crater density must reconcile with resurfacing.** Young surfaces require a process that removed or buried older craters.
10. **Every external source remains citation-only until asset review.**
