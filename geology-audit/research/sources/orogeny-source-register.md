# Collision and orogeny source register

## Scope

This register supports the Stage 1 research modules for:

- continent–continent collision;
- fold-and-thrust wedges;
- foreland basins;
- plateaus and syntaxes;
- gravitational collapse;
- lower-crustal flow;
- delamination, foundering, and slab breakoff;
- ancient, eroded, buried, and reactivated orogens.

The evidence-state labels follow `source-register.md`.

## Source-use rules added by this tranche

1. **Collision inherits ocean closure.** Arc, accretionary, passive-margin, terrane, and transform histories must remain available to the audit.
2. **A suture is not automatically a ridge.** It is a lineage and mechanical-contrast field whose visible expression depends on later processes.
3. **Convergence does not map directly to elevation.** Shortening partition, rheology, underthrusting, erosion, and support mechanisms must be separate.
4. **Foreland systems are structural and sedimentary systems.** Structural depth, fill, water depth, and surface elevation cannot be merged.
5. **Plateaus require support histories.** Crustal, lithospheric, dynamic, magmatic, and removal-related mechanisms may coexist or change through time.
6. **Extension can coexist with convergence.** Syn-convergent normal faulting is not automatically a contradiction.
7. **Delamination and breakoff are transient and uncertain.** They cannot become generic radial uplift stamps.
8. **Structural age differs from landform age.** Old orogenic fabrics may underlie younger uplift, incision, glaciation, or rifting.
9. **Ancient belts retain source-to-sink evidence.** Sediment systems and matching provinces may preserve the orogen after relief fades.
10. **No single Earth orogen is universal.** Himalaya, Alps, Andes/Puna, Appalachians, and other systems each constrain different branches.
