# Volcanism and magmatic-province source register

## Scope

This register supports Stage 1 research for:

- magma generation, transport, storage, and eruption style;
- shields, composite volcanoes, cones, domes, calderas, and collapse;
- volcanic loading, flexure, erosion, and burial;
- hotspots, plume-like systems, swells, and age-progressive chains;
- large igneous provinces, flood basalts, oceanic plateaus, and volcanic margins;
- comparative volcanism on Earth, Venus, Mars, Io, the Moon, and Mercury;
- valid radial structures and procedural-failure controls;
- generator obligations for volcanic state, history, material budgets, and downstream consequences.

The evidence-state labels follow `source-register.md`.

**Every source in this file is citation-only during Stage 1. No figure, map, photograph, radar product, or dataset is approved for Stage 2 use without a separate asset-specific licensing decision.**

## Source-use rules established by this tranche

1. **Volcanism is a source-to-surface history.** Activity scalars cannot replace melt source, transport, storage, vent, construction, and preservation state.
2. **Magma supply is not erupted volume.** Intrusive and extrusive fractions must remain separate.
3. **Edifice categories are outcomes, not source classes.** Similar source settings can produce different forms, and similar forms can arise from different histories.
4. **Calderas are collapse systems.** They require withdrawal/storage relations, structural failure, and later fill/resurgence history.
5. **Collapse has a mass destination.** Sector removal must feed debris, sediment, water displacement, or an explicit exported fraction.
6. **Hotspot tracks record relative motion.** Sources are not universally fixed, and chains are not perfect bead sequences.
7. **Broad volcanic swells and radial forms can be valid.** Scale, support, history, material budget, and downstream response must distinguish them from procedural blobs.
8. **LIPs are distributed provinces.** Fissures, flows, dikes, sills, underplating, pulses, and later fragmentation matter more than one central edifice.
9. **Planetary analogues require regime labels.** Gravity-only resizing and Earth-atmosphere assumptions are invalid.
10. **Visible volcanic density is not activity alone.** Burial, erosion, recycling, impacts, and mapping resolution govern preservation.
11. **Volcanism modifies other systems.** Crust, load/flexure, surface age, drainage, sediment, ocean exposure, and sometimes climate must receive explicit consequences.
12. **Every source remains citation-only until asset review.** Scientific authority does not imply image/data reuse rights.
